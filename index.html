00 · What is this

A real MUD, hiding inside a terminal clubhouse

late.sh is a cozy SSH terminal clubhouse — lofi beats, casual games, chat, and a small warm community, all reachable with a single ssh command and no software to install. Lateania is the door game living inside it: a persistent fantasy world you walk room by room, fight your way through, and slowly make your own.

It plays like the golden-age MUDs — but it doesn't insist on staying stuck in 1994. Rooms render as rich, painterly prose; a live overhead minimap tracks every step you've taken; a walk-around biome field view lets you see foes, tameable beasts, and gathering nodes around you at a glance; and every screen works whether you live on the keyboard, click with a mouse, or want both at once.

It has been growing for months — the living world arc, four capitals, a procedurally-built Frontier of twenty zones, three whole endgame continents, a full crafting economy, taming and mounts, and most recently Wildbound, the expansion that pushed the level cap to 100. Nobody is paying anybody to build this. It gets built because it's fun to build, one late night at a time.

at a glance

  • 🌍8,555 rooms — from the King's Road out to three procedurally-carved endgame continents and a shattered archipelago.
  • 🗡️5,056 monster spawns, 139 named bosses, each with real drop tables.
  • 🧭A live minimap and walk-around field view — every drawn line is a road you can actually walk.
  • ⌨️Keyboard, mouse, or hybrid — pick your control style, everything is clickable either way.
  • 🏡Player housing, from a wattle hut to a wizard's tower.

01 · Make your character

Seventeen classes, seventeen ways to fight

No rigid ancestries here — every class is open to any look you like, built trait by trait (build, hair, eyes, bearing, and more) into a bio the world reads back to you. What actually shapes how you play is the class: its own resource, its own passive edge, and a hundred levels of abilities to unlock.

05 · The latest expansion

Wildbound — the mounted expansion

Lateania's biggest single update: the level cap doubled, the trades grew a sixth tier, taming got its rideable summit, and the world's oldest map bug — phantom corridors that looked walkable but weren't — finally got its iron rule: a drawn line always means a road.

  • 📈Level cap 50 → 100. All seventeen classes gained ten new abilities apiece across the new levels — 170 in all — each capped off with a level-100 capstone: Worldbreaker, Supernova, Divine Reckoning, Killing Silence, Skyfall Arrow, Nature's Wrath, Harvest of Souls, Finale, Fist of Transcendence, Final Crusade, Oblivion Pact, Ragnarok Blow, Apex Strike, Songs End, Master Glyph, Vale's Verdict, and Soulstorm.
  • ⚒️A sixth trade tier. Every gathering and crafting skill now climbs to 100, ending in legendary-rarity gear gathered and forged from the far lands.
  • 🐎Rideable mounts. Ten new beasts across the doubled taming ladder, five wild and five mythical, every one saddle-ready.
  • 🗺️The phantom-corridor fix. The minimap used to draw a line wherever an exit existed, even when the world's folds put the real destination somewhere else. Every one of the world's ~154,000 map connectors was swept and verified — 495 phantoms found and fixed, zero left.
  • 📊An honest difficulty gradient. Doubling the cap could have left every endgame foe reading as a flat "level 100." Instead the whole Frontier → Reaches → Kaelmyr arc now climbs in a real, legible curve from the low 60s to the true summit.

06 · Get in the world

No client. No download. Just SSH.

  1. Open a terminal.
    macOS, Linux, or Windows — anything with an SSH client, which is to say almost anything.
  2. Connect.
    ssh late.sh — no account creation up front, no install, no port-forwarding.
  3. Pick your interaction mode.
    Keyboard (mouse fully off, so native terminal copy/paste just works), Mouse (click everything, Discord-like), or Hybrid — asked once on first login, changeable anytime in Settings.
  4. Find the Games hub, choose Lateania.
    Roll a class, name your character, and step out of Embergate onto the King's Road.

while you play

  • 🧭Press m for the world atlas, or toggle the live walk-around field view for a real sense of place.
  • 🧪Q quaffs the right potion mid-fight without breaking your view of the battle.
  • 🐎G swings you into the saddle the moment you've tamed something worth riding.
  • 🖱️Everything on screen is click-through in Mouse or Hybrid mode — the action bar, the map, targets, menus.
